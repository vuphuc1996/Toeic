## Scoring rubric

Score each criterion from 0 to 2:
- **0 — Fail:** incorrect, unclear, unnatural or not suitable for A2.
- **1 — Needs revision:** mostly understandable but contains ambiguity, awkward language, weak distractor or unclear audio cue.
- **2 — Pass:** natural, unambiguous, level-appropriate and supported by the dialogue.

Review each clip for:
1. **Naturalness:** conversation sounds plausible for a workplace/daily-life situation; speaker turns connect naturally.
2. **A2 suitability:** vocabulary, grammar, sentence length and implied knowledge are reasonable for A2 learners.
3. **Q1 detail:** exactly one correct answer, directly supported by an audible detail.
4. **Q2 purpose/context:** tests why/for whom/under what context, not merely repeats Q1.
5. **Q3 inference/next action:** inference is reasonable and sufficiently supported; no unsupported assumption.
6. **Distractors:** three plausible but clearly incorrect alternatives; no duplicate, grammatical clue or answer-length giveaway.
7. **Audio/TTS clarity:** names, numbers, times and key words are intelligible with the configured voices and pauses.

**Approval rule:** No critical criterion may score 0. Any correction must be recorded against the clip/question ID. A reviewer should listen to the audio, not approve from transcript alone.
