# Foundation B1 — Curriculum Blueprint (v2.92.0)

**Status:** DESIGN BASELINE — NOT IMPLEMENTED AS A LEARNER PATHWAY.

## Purpose

Define a measurable B1 bridge from the current A2 pathway without presenting a stage selector entry as a completed course. B1 should move learners from controlled everyday communication to independent handling of routine workplace and service situations.

## Proposed structure

- 12 units across workplace communication, scheduling, customer support, purchasing, travel, operations, reporting, recruitment, finance, digital tools, problem resolution and cross-team coordination.
- Four integrated skills per unit: Listening, Reading, Speaking and Writing. TOEIC Listening/Reading practice remains a separate exam-preparation layer.
- Each unit: 1 measurable outcome, 1 language focus, 1 contextual vocabulary set, 1 listening resource, 1 reading resource, 1 speaking task, 1 writing task, 6–8 objective questions and one delayed-recall checkpoint.
- Target language: approximately B1 CEFR descriptor range as an editorial design guide only; no CEFR certification claim.

## Draft outcome map

| Unit | Context | Observable outcome |
|---|---|---|
| B1-01 | Workplace routines | Explain a routine and sequence its steps |
| B1-02 | Scheduling | Negotiate a meeting time and confirm changes |
| B1-03 | Customer support | Clarify a problem and propose a next step |
| B1-04 | Purchasing | Compare quotations and explain a recommendation |
| B1-05 | Business travel | Handle a booking change and report an issue |
| B1-06 | Operations | Describe a process and a safety requirement |
| B1-07 | Reports | Summarize a trend and support it with details |
| B1-08 | Recruitment | Describe experience and respond to interview prompts |
| B1-09 | Finance | Explain a payment, invoice or expense discrepancy |
| B1-10 | Digital tools | Give instructions and troubleshoot a common issue |
| B1-11 | Problem resolution | Explain cause, impact and corrective action |
| B1-12 | Cross-team coordination | Deliver a concise update and agree responsibilities |

## Evidence and acceptance

- Listening/Reading: objective item evidence with source-grounded answer explanations; require at least two distinct contexts per skill across the stage.
- Speaking: learner-recorded or typed planning notes plus a self-reflection rubric; do not claim automatic pronunciation assessment unless a validated local capability is added.
- Writing: saved first draft, revision and rubric self-rating; no automatic CEFR grading.
- Progress: separate evidence scopes per unit and skill, preserving existing local stores. Implementation must reuse existing storage contracts unless a separately approved migration is required.
- Editorial gate: every new item marked pending until a second reviewer records item-level decisions.
- Learner gate: pilot with learners, revise ambiguous instructions and validate workload before marking B1 ready.

## Explicit exclusions

No B1 lessons are added to the stage selector in v2.92.0. This blueprint does not certify CEFR level, add server services, upload learner data, or alter IndexedDB v1.
