## Why this is a major quality release

The release reduces the amount of generic placeholder English explanation in existing reference terms by reusing canonical bilingual headword senses. It adds a guarded tool, provenance, regression checks and an audit spanning vocabulary, glossary, practice inventory and learning-outcome instrumentation. It does not claim external dictionary review or learner mastery.
